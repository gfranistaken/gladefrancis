My wife runs a CPA firm. Every practice management tool on the market got in her way during tax season, so she walked me through the pain points and I built CPA Relay to her specs.

It handles the work around the return: chasing clients for documents, getting engagement letters and 8879s signed, tracking who owes what, and billing for it. It doesn't calculate taxes, e-file, or replace the general ledger. Firms keep Drake, QuickBooks, or whatever they already trust for that.

## Who it's for

Solo practitioners and firms with 1 to 15 people. These firms are too small for enterprise practice suites and too busy to run the season out of email threads and a shared drive.

## What it does

- **Engagement letters.** 53 built-in templates, including 7216 consents, with merge fields that fill in client details.
- **Document collection.** Reusable PBC checklists, client uploads (phone photos included), and automatic reminders when items go overdue.
- **E-signatures.** Form 8878 and 8879 signing with recorded identity verification and a SHA-256 audit certificate. Spouses who share an email address get separate signing sessions.
- **Client portal.** One login per client, even across several entities, and a home screen that shows only what the client needs to do next.
- **What's Outstanding.** One firm-wide board of open tasks, signatures, approvals, messages, and unpaid invoices.
- **Time, proposals, and billing.** Time logged against partner and staff rates, staged proposals, invoices, and Stripe payments by ACH, card, or Cash App Pay.

Client-visible folders and messages are amber. Staff-only items are sky blue. Nobody has to guess what the client can see.
